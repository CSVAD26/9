The International Commission on Illumination (CIE) supplies the unmodified local tables **CIE 1931 colour-matching functions, 2 degree observer**, DOI [10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b](https://doi.org/10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b), and **CIE standard illuminant D65**, DOI [10.25039/CIE.DS.hjfjmt59](https://doi.org/10.25039/CIE.DS.hjfjmt59). They and adapted data are under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), distinct from the application's code license. Source URLs, original-byte digests and processing are recorded in [provenance.json](data/cie/provenance.json). Parsing, row selection and relative illumination normalization produce derived representations; they add no measured resolution. The authoring system deliberately sets reflectance outside 380–780 nm to zero. This is an authored support convention, not measured data. The complete CIE notice is distributed in `public/notices/cie-colorimetry.txt`.

Color-conversion methods follow the [W3C CSS Color 4 reference conversion equations](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-color-4/#color-conversion-code), [Björn Ottosson's Oklab](https://bottosson.github.io/posts/oklab/), and the CIEDE2000 equations from Sharma, Wu and Dalal (2005), [implementation notes and test data](https://hajim.rochester.edu/ece/sites/gsharma/ciede2000/). The retained numerical code implements the equations rather than redistributing their MATLAB or spreadsheet programs.

Microwave sonification implements pulsar synthesis: a windowed waveform packet followed by real silence, with separately controlled repetition and packet duration. Method credit: Curtis Roads, [Sound Composition with Pulsars](https://www.curtisroads.net/articles-1), and [Pulsar Synthesis Revisited, section 3](https://cmmr2012.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/sites/cmmr2012.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/files/pdf/papers/cmmr2012_submission_121.pdf). Radio sonification implements a damped ideal string through eight harmonic modes and spatial excitation/observation coupling; method credit: Julius O. Smith III, [modal representation in Physical Audio Signal Processing](https://dsprelated.com/freebooks/pasp/Modal_Representation.html). These are independent implementations of the synthesis methods. The curve/image-to-parameter mappings, seeded excitation and eight-second scan are authored for this application; they are artistic translations rather than electromagnetic recordings.
