# Impossible Colors · Distributed credits

Course interaction design: CSVAD26 [PaletteExplorer](https://github.com/CSVAD26/code_samples/tree/3303b224955a32c9ac7c18c997e5c548860864ff/colorp5/PaletteExplorer), MAT 236. The full spectrum picker extends this coursework with authored curves, local image effects and synthesis.

p5.js 1.11.13 (LGPL-2.1) and vgpu 0.5.0 (MIT) are credited with their license texts in [bundled-dependencies.md](bundled-dependencies.md). The jump-flood/cascade shader adaptation retains [vgpu's MIT notice](LICENSE.vgpu.txt). Visual references: vgpu [Radiance Cascades](https://vgpu.sh/examples/radiance-cascades), [Depth Estimation](https://vgpu.sh/examples/depth-estimation), [Agent Radiance Cascades](https://vgpu.sh/examples/agent-radiance-cascades), and the selective glitches in [Glitchy Structure](https://discourse.threejs.org/t/glitchy-structure/94619).

CIE 1931 2° colour-matching functions and CIE standard illuminant D65 are attributed to the International Commission on Illumination and distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. The tables are retained unmodified in the source distribution; color integration derives parsed, row-selected and normalized representations. [cie-colorimetry.txt](cie-colorimetry.txt) contains the full attribution, DOI and licensing notice. Authored zero support outside 380–780 nm is a design convention, not measured data.

FastDepth derives from dwofk/fast-depth, Copyright (c) 2019 Diana Wofk, MIT, via PINTO Model Zoo #146 and the audited vgpu model mirror. The 5,420,454-byte model's SHA-256 is `dfc532a08f0ee34283d890d845e3824973f17240ad1d7eb617d9959ec8dc23c9`. Its [provenance](../models/depth/PROVENANCE.md) and [license](../models/depth/LICENSE) accompany the model. It estimates visible-surface depth rather than internal structure. ONNX Runtime Web 1.27.0 is MIT-licensed, Copyright (c) Microsoft Corporation; its [license](../models/depth/runtime/LICENSE), [additional notices](../models/depth/runtime/ThirdPartyNotices.txt) and local asset manifest accompany the runtime.

Microwave pulsar synthesis follows Curtis Roads' [Sound Composition with Pulsars](https://www.curtisroads.net/articles-1) and [Pulsar Synthesis Revisited, section 3](https://cmmr2012.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/sites/cmmr2012.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/files/pdf/papers/cmmr2012_submission_121.pdf): independently controlled waveform packets and real silence. Radio implements eight modes of a damped ideal string with excitation/observation coupling, following Julius O. Smith III's [modal representation](https://dsprelated.com/freebooks/pasp/Modal_Representation.html). These are independent implementations of the methods; the image/curve parameter mapping and eight-second scan are authored artistic interpretations, not electromagnetic recordings.
