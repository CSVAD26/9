# Reflections

Use this folder for your own reading notes and reflection drafts. The official reading responses and peer comments belong in the slide decks or other destinations linked from the [course schedule](https://csvad26.github.io/course_site/schedule).

For each note, record the reading title, author, and date, followed by your own analysis, questions, or connections to your practice. Quote sparingly and identify the source of quotations.

The course requires reading responses to be your own writing. This folder contains no completed responses. Check the current schedule and instructor guidance for submission deadlines.
