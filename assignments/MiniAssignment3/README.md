# Mini Assignment 3 · Image Annotation

[Assignment brief](https://csvad26.github.io/course_site/assignments/mini-assignment-3) · [Course examples](https://github.com/CSVAD26/code_samples/tree/main/layoutp5)

**Status: starter — replace this line when you begin.**

Modify an example to take an image or video as input and subdivide it, add composition lines, or highlight color regions. You may combine approaches.

## Work to complete

- Implement your chosen approach in `sketch.js`.
- Put shareable input images in `assets/` and identify their sources.
- Explain the input and any controls; save results in `documentation/`.
- Commit and push the work in `assignments/MiniAssignment3/`.

## Your documentation

Replace these prompts with your own account:

- **Starting point and credit:** Which example and media did you use?
- **Method:** How does the sketch annotate or divide its input?
- **Interaction:** What can a viewer change?
- **Reflection:** What worked, and what are its limitations?
